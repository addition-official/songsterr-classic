Songsterr Classic Settings

Keep Classic on in new tabs

When this is on, switching Classic on in one tab turns it on for every Songsterr song you open after that, including new tabs and after restarting your browser. Switching it off in the popup turns it off for new tabs again.

Classic still only starts on song pages, never on account or payment pages. Tabs that are already open keep their current mode until you switch them or reload them.
